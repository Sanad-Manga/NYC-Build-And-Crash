Which boroughs account for a larger share of collisions than of construction, and has that changed?

Measured with new buildings, Manhattan has the largest positive gap (collision share minus new-building share), +14.78 percentage points on average over 2022–2025, and Staten Island the largest negative gap (−24.39). Brooklyn stays positive (+9.69 in 2022, +10.51 in 2025), while the Bronx moves from +4.98 to −2.73 and Staten Island narrows from −23.27 to −14.07. Partial 2026 (January–October) looks similar and is not directly comparable to a full year. This is an association only: shares count only crashes with a ZIP (about 28% are excluded), and there is no traffic or population denominator.
