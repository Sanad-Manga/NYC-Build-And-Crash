How deadly are the crashes in ZIP codes with a lot of new construction?

Crashes in ZIPs with the most new construction were not clearly deadlier. The top quartile had 2.18 deaths per 1,000 collisions (95% interval 1.93–2.45; 221 deaths / 101,334 collisions) against 2.40 for the bottom quartile (1.30–3.27; 24 / 10,001). For pedestrians and cyclists the rates were 1.22 (1.03–1.42) and 1.50 (0.67–2.30). Both intervals overlap, so the difference could be chance. This is an association only: about 28% of crashes have no ZIP and are excluded, and there is no traffic or population denominator, so these are deaths per reported crash, not a resident's risk.
