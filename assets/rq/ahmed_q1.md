Are ZIP codes with more new buildings also the ZIP codes where pedestrians and cyclists are hurt more often per collision?

Across the 172 ZIP codes we could analyse (2022-2025, full years only), the 44 ZIPs in the top quartile for new buildings (at least 47 new-building jobs) had 15.5 pedestrian and cyclist injuries per 100 collisions (95% interval 14.3 to 16.7). The 43 ZIPs in the bottom quartile (at most 9.75 jobs) had 21.4 per 100 collisions (95% interval 19.2 to 23.5). The Spearman correlation between new buildings and this rate is -0.325 (permutation p < 0.001): the association is negative, while new buildings are positively correlated with the number of collisions (0.427) and with raw injuries (0.200), because busy ZIPs have both more building and more traffic. This is an association only; there is no traffic or pedestrian-count data, about 28% of crashes have no ZIP and are excluded, and 2026 (January to October) is left out. Source: phase3_analysis_ahmed.ipynb.
